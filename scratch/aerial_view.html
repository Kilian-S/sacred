What you are looking at (game v2). Each sortie the enemy secretly commits K hazards to candidate positions; the UAV then flies base → target on one smooth, bank-limited curve drawn from the selected strategy. Inside a hazard’s radius the flight accrues hazard RATE proportional to proximity; survival is exp(−∫rate ds) along the curve, calibrated so a straight transit through the centre is intercepted with probability exactly pmax. The glow on the flight path is that accrual, computed live from the same integral the oracle uses, so the empirical rates converge to the predicted values in the table. What training does: SACRED replaces the fixed strategy with a policy network that reads the threat field and outputs the route mixture, sparring against a fictitious-play enemy best-responding to its recent flights; the pre-registered bar (experiments/gen28_aerial.md, v2): beat the inverse-risk lane rule, including on threat layouts the policy has never seen.
