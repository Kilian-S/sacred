What this is. A procedurally generated contested theatre: mountains (obstacles, forming valleys), an urban core and a river the interdictor cannot emplace in (cover corridors), open fields (long-range exposure), forest (short-range ambush), roads (mobile-SAM danger lines). Base → target is ~50 km. Three drones resupply together; the mission FAILS if any one is intercepted. The enemy secretly commits one hazard, best-responding to your strategy; interception accrues continuously with proximity along each drone’s flight (the same line integral the oracle scores). Every number is exact, no training has been run — this is the instance for your review. Notice: any single fixed route (direct or corridor) is ~100% mission failure (the enemy just sits on it); only a calibrated mixture survives, and the gap between the strongest naive spread and the equilibrium is what a trained policy would have to capture.
