## 4. THE SHORTEST HONEST SUMMARY OF WHERE THE RESEARCH STANDS

Four positives are banked across the programme: the negative campaign with its mechanism chain;
the dynamic register (gen19/27/31/32, where learning beats every static object); gen30's
oracle-priced facility-location frontier; and now gen39's concealment mechanic plus the LLM
composition positive. Two hard boundaries bound all of it: **a two-line rule is near-optimal
wherever it has somewhere safe to go** (and in gen39's register no trained policy beat the best
observing rule at all), and **the conditioning-capacity wall** sighted three times (gen29
coordination, gen34 type, gen39 exposure), where a channel is present, visible to the
architecture, and the policy fails to convert it while simple rules use it fine.

What gen39 adds that is genuinely new: **a measured account of where a language model helps in
this pipeline** - it composes terrain-grounded forces that beat a hand-tuned doctrine against a
searching defender, it authors a curriculum that fixes a training negative and transfers to
unseen maps, and its distinctive advantage is *sample efficiency at small evaluation budgets*,
not raw quality. Everything is per-model, and the two models swap places between tasks.
